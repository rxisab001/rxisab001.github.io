Privacy & SMS Terms

This page explains why you may receive text messages or emails from your landlord through this system, what kinds of messages to expect, and how to stop receiving them at any time.

What We Send SMS Terms Your Privacy Contact

01 — What We Send

Messages You May Receive

As a tenant, you may receive SMS text messages and emails related to your tenancy. All messages come directly from your landlord and are sent only for the purposes listed below. You will never receive promotional or marketing content.

Rent Reminders

A friendly reminder sent a few days before your rent is due. It will include the amount owed and the due date. These are sent to help you avoid late fees — not as a demand notice.

Late Payment Notices

If a rent payment has not been received after the grace period, you will receive a single notice letting you know the payment is overdue. This message is sent once per late payment.

Payment Confirmations

A confirmation sent when your rent payment has been recorded, so you have a record that it was received.

Planned Maintenance & Property Notices

Advance notice of any scheduled maintenance, inspections, repairs, or other property events that may affect your unit or require access. These are sent as early as possible to give you adequate notice.

Lease Renewal Reminders

A heads-up when your lease is approaching its end date, so you have time to discuss renewal or make other arrangements.

Welcome & Move-In Messages

An introductory message sent at the start of your tenancy with any relevant information for getting settled.

That's it. You will only ever receive messages related to your tenancy. No promotions, no third-party offers, no spam.

02 — SMS Messaging Terms

Text Message Program

Opt-In

By providing your phone number to your landlord, you consent to receive the transactional text messages described above. Your consent is not a condition of your lease or tenancy.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on your payment schedule and property activity. In a typical month you can expect one to three messages — usually a rent reminder and, if applicable, a payment confirmation. Maintenance and lease messages are sent only when relevant.

Message & Data Rates

Standard message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier plan. There is no charge from us for any messages sent through this program.

How to Opt Out

You may stop receiving text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message. You can also use any of the following keywords: STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, or QUIT. You will receive one final confirmation message and no further messages will be sent to your number. To resume messages, contact your landlord directly.

Help

Reply HELP to any message for assistance, or contact Ryan Isabelle directly using the details in the Contact section below.

Supported Carriers

This program is available on most major US carriers. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

03 — Your Privacy

How Your Information Is Used

Your phone number and email address are used solely to send the tenancy-related messages described above. They are stored securely and are never used for any other purpose.

No sharing, ever. No mobile information — including your phone number and SMS opt-in data — will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. This applies at all times and without exception.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information held about you at any time. Simply contact Ryan Isabelle using the details below and your request will be addressed promptly.
